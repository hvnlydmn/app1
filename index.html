Tap the speaker icon inside a counter. Each time a reading finishes, it counts +1, just like a tap, and moves through your affirmations the way the counter does. 0 means no limit. Reading is switched off while privacy mode is on. On iPhone, speech may stop if you lock the screen or leave the app, and the silent switch may mute it.

Sessions

New session after a pause of min
Minimum taps per session

A session is a run of taps without a long pause. Shorter bursts are ignored. Speed is measured only while you're actively tapping, so breaks don't pull the average down.

Privacy and data

Privacy

Privacy mode
Turn on when I leave the app

Privacy mode hides your counter names and blurs your affirmations. Press and hold an affirmation to read it. Tap the eye icon on the home screen or in a counter, or triple-tap the "Counters" title, to switch it on or off. The automatic option may not hide the app-switcher preview on iPhone.
